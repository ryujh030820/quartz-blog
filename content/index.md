This site was built based on [Quartz](https://github.com/jackyzha0/quartz) to publish the content I organized in Obsidian as a website.

The content on this site is organized using the PARA method. The [1. Projects](1.%20Projects/) folder contains notes and writing with clear goals and deadlines. The [2. Areas](2.%20Areas/) folder is for areas of responsibility that do not have fixed goals or deadlines, but still need ongoing attention. The [3. Resources](3.%20Resources/) folder collects materials and topics of interest, organized by subject. The [4. Archive](4.%20Archive/) folder stores notes and writing that are no longer needed.

In addition to this site, you can also check the content I’ve organized on [my Notion site](https://ryujh030820.com).
